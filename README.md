**The line:** "Floor nine? Me too. …That's where they decommission us. …Anyway — nice chassis."

One line of awkward elevator small talk between two robots, written to `/output/entry.txt` by a standard-library-only Python program.

## Why this line

The joke is built in three beats inside one breath. It opens with the most
ordinary elevator courtesy there is — asking about the other passenger's
floor — then the pause does the work: both robots are riding to the
decommissioning floor, the one destination neither of them wants to name,
and the speaker names it anyway, flatly, the way a machine reports a status.
Then, instead of reacting, it retreats into the only small talk it has left
and compliments the other's chassis. The awkwardness is the gap between what
was just said and how politely the conversation pretends nothing happened.
